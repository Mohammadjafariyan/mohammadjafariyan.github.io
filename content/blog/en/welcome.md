For more than eight years I've built software that has to work in production: investment platforms, a national telecom CRM, healthcare booking systems, and industrial monitoring. Each project taught me something that never ends up in the final code.

This blog is where I'll write those lessons down.

## What to expect

- **Backend and architecture:** API design, data modelling, and the trade-offs behind them in .NET and Node.js.
- **Delivery:** containers, Kubernetes, CI/CD, and monitoring that helps when something breaks.
- **Applied AI:** building LLM agents, RAG pipelines, and MCP integrations that connect to real systems.
- **Working across domains:** what fintech, telecom, healthcare, and industrial software have in common, and where they differ.

Some posts will be in English and some in Persian.
